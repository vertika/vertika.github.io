about

Hi, I'm Vertika — a dancer, choreographer, and teacher. I've spent most of my life in movement, and what keeps drawing me back is the simple, impossible question at the center of it all: what does it mean to be in a body?

I trained in classical forms and found my way into contemporary, improvisation, and everything in between. These days I split my time between creating work, teaching classes, and staying genuinely curious about what dance can be.

This is where I keep notes, share classes, and think out loud. Glad you're here.
